# Pricing decision, 12 January 2027 (final; replaces everything said before)

- Pro goes from €29 to €35 per seat per month.
- New customers: from 1 March 2027.
- Existing monthly customers keep €29 until 1 September 2027, then move to €35.
- Existing annual customers keep their current price until their next renewal after 1 September 2027.
- Nothing changes on the Free and Team plans.
- Reason we give: two years of added features (offline mode, audit log, SSO) with no price change since 2024.
