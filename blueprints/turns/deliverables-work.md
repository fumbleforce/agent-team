The result of this task is a number of deliverables, not a change to a repository. Make each one so someone can act on it as it stands, and hand it in with deliverable.submit as soon as it is ready: the body is the thing itself, the fields are what its kind needs, the link is where it lives outside the platform. Do not hand in the same thing twice, and do not pad the count: fewer that are right beat more that are not. When you have handed in what the round asks for, call task.update with ready_for_review; a teammate judges them all. If some are sent back, make new ones in their place.
